Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a real CMRRL product?

No. It is an independent concept inspired by the Children's Media Research and Reform Lab's focus, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Lab.

Are the organizations and research real?

No. Every organization, person, publication and number is fictional demonstration data.

Does it give parenting or medical advice?

No. Materials are educational and reflective. Questions about a child's health or development belong with a qualified professional.

Can I try it?

Yes. Open the interactive prototype to explore every screen.
